Introduction: There's Another
Way to Build an Online Business

Have you ever wanted to start an online business but felt held back because you don't have your own product to sell?

Perhaps you've thought about the money, time, and effort required to create a product, manage inventory, package orders, and handle deliveries.

Here's something you should know: you don't necessarily need to create your own product to start an online business.

There's a business model that allows you to promote products created by other businesses and earn a commission when people purchase through your unique affiliate link.

It's called affiliate marketing.

It gives beginners an opportunity to learn online promotion, customer acquisition, and digital sales without having to develop their own products.

What Exactly Is Affiliate Marketing?

Affiliate marketing is a business model where you earn commissions by promoting products or services belonging to other businesses.

Here's a simple example:

Imagine a business has a product selling for ₦100,000 and offers affiliates a ₦30,000 commission for every successful sale.

Your role is to promote that product to potential customers. When someone purchases through your unique affiliate link, you earn the agreed commission.

You don't have to manufacture the product yourself. The business provides the product, while you focus on connecting potential buyers with it.

That's the basic idea behind affiliate marketing.

Why Are More People Becoming
Interested in Affiliate Marketing?

There are several reasons people are exploring this business model.

1. Freedom

Affiliate marketing gives you the flexibility to work from different locations and manage your business activities around your schedule.

2. Independence

You can work towards building an additional income stream instead of depending entirely on one source of income.

3. It's a Lifetime Skill

You'll have the opportunity to develop valuable digital skills such as marketing, customer acquisition, sales, persuasion, communication, and product promotion.

These are skills you can continue developing and applying in different areas of business.

4. You Don't Need to Create Your Own Product

You can promote products that already exist instead of spending time developing your own product before getting started.

5. You Can Operate Using Your Smartphone

You can learn and manage many affiliate marketing activities using a smartphone and an internet connection.

6. You Can Leverage Social Media

Platforms like Facebook, WhatsApp, Instagram, and other social media channels can help you reach people who may be interested in the products you promote.

7. You Can Start Learning as a Complete Beginner

You don't need to come into affiliate marketing knowing everything already. You can learn the fundamentals, understand the process, and develop your skills as you go.

Real People. Real Action. Real Sales.

These are examples shared by people involved in the process. Results vary, and affiliate marketing does not guarantee income.
